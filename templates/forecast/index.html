Recursive Forecasting Limitations

Error Compounding: Predictions at step $T+1$ are fed as lag inputs to step $T+2$. Over 30 days, early forecast inaccuracies compound progressively.

Regression toward Mean: Extended multi-step horizons tend to smooth out extreme consumption spikes in favour of the historical mean.

Unforeseen Externalities: Epidemic outbreaks or regulatory shifts cannot be anticipated by historical periodicity alone without clinical telemetry.
